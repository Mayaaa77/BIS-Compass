Visual Detection

Spot Fake & Counterfeit Marks

How to distinguish a genuine BIS certification mark from counterfeit knock-offs.

✅ GENUINE ISI MARK CHECKLIST
  • Contains exact Indian Standard number above the mark (e.g. IS 17803).
  • Contains valid 7 or 10-digit CM/L license number directly below the mark.
  • Geometric proportions adhere precisely to the official BIS monogram standard.
❌ COMMON COUNTERFEIT WARNING SIGNS
  • ISI mark printed without any CM/L license number underneath.
  • Contains words like "Approved by BIS" or "Standard Quality" instead of the official mark.
  • Misaligned or stretched lettering in the central "S" shape.
