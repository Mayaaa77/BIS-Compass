# BIS Compass | National Standards, Compliance & Universal Verification Portal

**BIS Compass** is an advanced, comprehensive digital portal designed for citizens, consumers, manufacturers, MSMEs, researchers, and students to navigate Indian Standards (IS), mandatory Quality Control Orders (QCO), and conformity assessments under the Bureau of Indian Standards (BIS Act, 2016).

---

## Key Features

### 1. Universal Barcode & QR Code Scanner
- **Dual-Engine Code Recognition**:
  - **1D Barcodes**: Decodes EAN-13 (GS1 India 890...), UPC-A, Code 128, EAN-8, and Code 39.
  - **2D QR Codes**: Decodes BIS Care App QR codes, Manakonline Digital Verification URLs, Gold Hallmark HUID QR codes, and GS1 Digital Link 2D QR codes.
- **Triple Input Modes**:
  - **Hardware Camera Viewfinder**: Continuous real-time frame scanning with target-lock and audio chime.
  - **Drag-and-Drop & Image Upload**: Upload or drop any barcode or QR code image/photo/screenshot directly onto the viewfinder.
  - **Live Typing & Handheld Scanner Gun**: Instant debounced lookup as you type, or press Enter to scan immediately.
- **Multi-Input Batch Scanning**: Enter 1, 2, 5, or 20+ codes separated by commas, spaces, or newlines to view side-by-side comparison tables.

### 2. Indian Standards & BIS Information Lookup
When any product barcode or QR code is scanned, the engine instantly queries and displays:
- **Applicable Indian Standard**: Full IS code, edition year, and official specification title (e.g. IS 17803:2022, IS 4151:2020, IS 14543:2016, IS 12269:2013, IS 16102:2012, IS 694:2010, IS 2347:2017, IS 1417:2016, etc.).
- **Mandatory QCO Regulatory Order**: Specific ministry order (DPIIT, MoRTH, Ministry of Steel, MeitY, FSSAI) mandating compliance under Section 16 of the BIS Act, 2016.
- **Certification Scheme**: Scheme-I (ISI Mark for products), Scheme-II (CRS for electronics & IT), or Scheme-IV (HUID Hallmarking for precious metals).
- **License / Certificate Details**: Active CM/L license number, CRS (R-) number, or 6-digit HUID.
- **Mandatory Laboratory Testing Checklist**: Mandated tests such as heavy metal leaching, thermal retention, drop impact deceleration, coliform counts, and alloy purity assays.
- **Official BIS Manakonline Verification**: Direct links pointing to manakonline.in.
- **Inspector Advice & Safety Verdict**: Genuine vs. counterfeit assessment and guidance.

### 3. 7 Integrated Portal Workspaces
1. **Overview & Universal Scanner**: Core mission, metrology laboratory overview, and universal barcode/QR code verification.
2. **Standards & QCO Intelligence**: Searchable repository of 21,000+ Indian Standards with dynamic sector filtering.
3. **Industry & MSME Estimator**: Interactive fee calculator with MSME concessions (Micro 80%, Small 50%).
4. **Citizens & Consumers Hub**: Entrepreneurial roadmaps, sample benchmarks, and consumer safety guides.
5. **Consumer Rights & Grievance**: Rights charter, misleading advertisement alerts, and grievance redressal workflows.
6. **Research & Metrology**: Technical committees, testing methodologies, and academic standardization resources.
7. **Students & Learners Hub**: Standards clubs, quiz games, student modules, and youth educational resources.

### 4. Trilingual Accessibility & Voice AI
- Instant language switching across **English**, **Hindi**, and **Telugu**.
- Text-to-speech engine providing automatic scan status announcements and manual detailed audio narration.
