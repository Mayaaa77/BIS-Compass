Spot Fake & Counterfeit Marks

How to distinguish a genuine BIS certification mark from counterfeit knock-offs.

✅ GENUINE ISI MARK CHECKLIST
  • Includes standard IS Code printed at the TOP (e.g. IS 17803).
  • Includes valid 7 to 10-digit CM/L Licence Number at the BOTTOM.
  • Precise geometric proportions of the central "ISI" monogram.
❌ FAKE MARK RED FLAGS
  • Missing CM/L licence number below the logo.
  • Generic phrases like "As per ISI Standards" or "ISI Tested" without a licence.
  • Distorted font geometry or blurry low-resolution packaging sticker.

Consumer Awareness

Safe Buying Checklists for Consumers

Mandatory hallmarks and certification marks to inspect before you pay.

💧 Packaged Drinking Water

Always look for IS 14543 or IS 13428 with a valid CM/L code. If the code is missing, it is illegal borewell water.

💍 Gold & Bullion Jewelry

Look for three stamps: BIS Triangle Logo, Purity mark (e.g. 22K916), and the laser 6-digit HUID code.

🪖 Two-Wheeler Helmets

Must carry IS 4151 with CM/L number. Non-ISI helmets fail impact attenuation tests and are legally banned.
