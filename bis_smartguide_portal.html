What is BIS and What Does It Do?

The Bureau of Indian Standards (BIS) is the National Standards Body of India, established under the BIS Act, 2016. Originally founded as the Indian Standards Institution (ISI) in 1947, BIS is responsible for harmonious development of the activities of standardization, marking, and quality certification of goods.

Key Student Takeaway: BIS protects consumer health, sets environmental specifications, and provides third-party quality guarantee via the ISI mark and HUID hallmark.
2

How Are Indian Standards Created?

Standards are developed through an open, transparent, multi-stakeholder 6-step consensus mechanism:

  1. Proposal: Any citizen, student, or industry suggests a new standard need.
  2. Sectional Committee: Technical experts, scientists, and academics draft specifications.
  3. Draft Publication: Public review period for 60 days on Manakonline.
  4. Consensus & Approval: Finalized technical clauses are approved.
  5. Gazette Notification: Officially published as an Indian Standard (IS).
3

BIS Standards Clubs in Schools & Colleges

Over 10,000 Standards Clubs have been formed in high schools and colleges across India to instill a culture of quality consciousness in young minds.

• Financial Grant: BIS provides an annual grant of ₹10,000 per club for learning activities, science quizzes, and industrial exposure visits.

• Mentorship: Science teachers are trained as club mentors to conduct practical experiments on food purity, helmet impact, and electrical safety.

4

Student Opportunities & Careers at BIS

BIS offers prestigious career pathways for science, technology, and engineering graduates:

• Scientist-B Recruitment: Entry-level Group A officers recruited based on GATE score across Civil, Mechanical, Electrical, Metallurgy, and Chemical engineering.

• Young Professionals (YP): Fixed-tenure policy, standardization, and laboratory metrology roles with attractive monthly stipends.

• University Internships: Live research projects on standardization in emerging fields like AI safety, EVs, and green hydrogen.
